Snagr · home screen proposal · v4 · motion and dialog polish

⌖Every item lives on a shelf

v4 adds motion to the shelves and redesigns the dialogs. Two designer agents reviewed v3, one for each, and each proposed three options. The switches above the mockup let you try every option. Both designers' recommended options are on by default.

v4 · shelvesSmooth open and closeThe body grows open in 200ms and closes in 160ms. The header never moves, so the page below slides instead of jumping. Rows drop in one after another.
v4 · shelvesStrikes point at the rowWhen an item lands in range, that row lights up, not the whole shelf. With reduced motion it gets a steady amber edge for 8 seconds instead of a flash that's easy to miss.
v4 · shelvesToggling doesn't redraw the pageOpening a shelf now changes only that shelf. Because v3 redrew the whole page on every click, its chevron animation never actually played.
v4 · dialogsField-card layoutHeader and footer stay fixed and only the middle scrolls, so the main button is always visible. There's a close ×, and dialogs animate in and out. On phones they open as a bottom sheet.
v4 · dialogsCompact site pickerOne-line rows with a monogram, the site's address and "N tracked". Paused sites are marked. ＋ Add a site that isn't listed opens a small form in place, and fills in the name from the URL.
v4 · dialogsFriendlier validationNo warnings before you've done anything. Buttons are never greyed out; errors appear when you click. Clicking outside won't throw away a half-filled form. Focus stays put while you pick sites.

Carried over: the v3 guide hero and the two-step New category (no item step), plus v2's collapsible shelves, urgency ordering and one-line rows.

What starts open

  • If 4 or fewer shelves hold your items, all of them start open.
  • With more than 4, only shelves with an item in range start open, plus the one holding the closest item.
  • Once you open or close shelves yourself, those choices win.

What reopens a shelf

  • A new item in range on a collapsed shelf. It opens without scrolling the page, so you aren't pulled away from what you're reading.
  • Adding an item to it. That shelf does scroll into view, because you just asked for it.
  • A search opens every shelf with a match. Ctrl-F also finds items inside collapsed shelves (hidden="until-found").

03Dialogs

This is the New category dialog, drawn right on the page in whichever style is picked above. The buttons open the other two dialogs inside the mockup, with their animations.

  • Header: an eyebrow line, the title and a close ×. The step pips match the hero's ① → ②. A finished step shows ✓ in grey, because green means a good price.
  • Site picker: one-line rows, with an amber edge and filled box when picked. The last row expands into an add-a-site form. A site you add is shared with everyone on the instance, and the form says so.
  • Buttons: the main button is always on the right and never greyed out. Clicking it with something missing shows an error under the field.
  • Phones: a bottom sheet with a grab bar. The buttons are 40px tall, and the main one is twice as wide.

04Still open

My default for each is in amber.

Which shelf motion?

Drawer is the plain, fast option. Lock-on adds hunter-style extras: a scan line and reticle brackets. Brackets on a strike feel earned. A scan line on every open might get tiring.

default: Drawer, plus Lock-on's brackets for strikes only
Which dialog style?

The field card works for all three dialogs. The two-pane preview teaches best, but it's too big for Add item and Edit sites. A mix is possible: two-pane only for your first category, when the hero sends you there.

default: Field card everywhere; two-pane only for the first category
Can people reorder shelves?

Urgency order puts what needs you first, but shelves move as prices change. Pinning is the middle ground.

default: urgency only for now
